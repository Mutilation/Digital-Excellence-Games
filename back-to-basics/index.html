Step right up! “Back to Basics” by Cody Savage is loaded. Or drop in any song and chop to the beat.

Hit Q W E R (or arrow keys) as notes cross the line. On a phone, tap the four lanes. Hold the key (or keep your finger down) for notes with a tail.
You start with 100 HP, and each missed note costs 1 HP. Esc pauses.
Combos of 25 / 50 / 75 / 100 / 200 each add +25% volume and a new act. Missing a note resets it.

CALIBRATE

Use the speakers or headphones you'll play with. You'll hear 4 count-in clicks, then tap any key (or click) on each of the next 12 clicks. Listen to the sound, not the screen.
